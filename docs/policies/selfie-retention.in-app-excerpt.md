We need a quick selfie to confirm you're a real person — it helps keep Tribely safe for everyone.

If approved, your selfie is deleted within 30 days. If we can't approve it, the photo is deleted within 7 days. Deleting your account deletes your selfie immediately.

By tapping Capture, you agree to Tribely collecting and reviewing this photo for identity verification.
