# Tribely Review Content Policy

**Effective date:** [to be set at publication]
**Applies to:** All reviews posted on Tribely (Singapore).
**Owner:** Tribely Trust & Safety.

## Purpose

Reviews on Tribely help solo travellers make informed decisions about whom to meet. Honest, specific feedback after a real shared event is what makes the platform trustworthy. This policy describes what is and is not acceptable in a review, and what Tribely does when a review crosses the line.

## When you can leave a review

You can leave a review only when:
1. You and the reviewed person were both approved participants in the same event.
2. The event status is "completed" on Tribely.
3. You have not previously left a review for that person for that event.

You have **24 hours** from submission to edit your review. After 24 hours your review is locked.

## What we expect from a review

A good review is:
- **Honest.** Based on what actually happened at the event.
- **Specific.** "Aaron showed up on time and was friendly" beats "great person."
- **Respectful.** Disagreement is fine; personal attacks are not.
- **About the event.** Not about someone's politics, looks, race, religion, sexuality, gender, nationality, or any other identity attribute.

## What is not allowed

Reviews must not contain:
- **Harassment or threats** — towards the reviewed person or anyone else.
- **Hate speech** — content attacking a person based on a protected characteristic.
- **Sexual content** — explicit material or unwanted sexual commentary.
- **Personal information** — addresses, workplaces, phone numbers, family details, or any data that could be used to locate or identify someone off-platform.
- **False information** — statements you know to be untrue, including allegations of crime that did not occur.
- **Spam** — promotional content, repeated identical text, off-topic material.
- **Impersonation or coordinated review manipulation.**

This list is not exhaustive. Tribely's moderators apply judgement.

## What happens when a review is reported

Any review can be reported by tapping the overflow icon on the review and choosing "Report." Reports are reviewed by Tribely within **72 hours**.

Possible outcomes:
- **Kept.** The review does not breach this policy; it remains visible.
- **Hidden.** The review breaches this policy; it is removed from the reviewed person's profile and excluded from their aggregate rating. The review author may still see their own submission but is notified that the review was hidden.

Tribely does not disclose individual moderation outcomes to reporters beyond the visibility of the reported review.

## Repeated violations

Authors who repeatedly post reviews that breach this policy may have their account restricted or removed. Tribely treats coordinated false-review campaigns as fraud and may report serious cases to law enforcement.

## Disagreeing with a moderation decision

If your review was hidden and you believe the decision was wrong, contact support@gotribely.com with the event ID and your reasoning. Tribely will respond within 14 days. External legal remedies (defamation proceedings, PDPC complaints) remain available to all users; Tribely's internal process is not a substitute.

## Defamation note

Tribely is the platform; the reviews are written by users. Tribely takes content concerns seriously, acts on reports promptly, and removes content that breaches this policy or Singapore law. We do not pre-publication-screen reviews. If you believe a review about you is defamatory under the Defamation Act 1957, you may report it to us and you may also seek independent legal advice.

## Changes

We will update this policy as Tribely grows. Material changes will be notified in-app.
